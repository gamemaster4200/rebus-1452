# Roadmap

- **v0.1 — Artificial Selection:** establish genomes, playback, and listener-guided selection.
- **v0.2 — Lineage + Hall of Fame:** preserve ancestry and notable organisms.
- **v0.3 — Self-Adaptive Mutation:** let mutation behavior evolve with the organism.
- **v0.4 — Transition Genome:** evolve relationships and movement between musical states.
- **v0.5 — Novelty Search:** reward meaningful difference in addition to preference.
- **v0.6 — Preference Model:** learn a listener's evolving aesthetic signals.
- **v0.7 — Species / Ecological Niches:** support distinct populations and pressures.
- **v0.8 — Autonomous Flock:** sustain evolving populations with less intervention.
- **v0.9 — Crowd Generation:** coordinate selection across many listeners.
- **v1.0 — Electric Sheep for Techno:** release the complete evolutionary music experience.
